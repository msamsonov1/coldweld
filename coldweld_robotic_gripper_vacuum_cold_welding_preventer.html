01. Contact Mechanics & Constriction Conductance

Holm-Sharvin

In terrestrial air, native oxide passivation layers ($\text{TiO}_2$, $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$, $t \approx 2\text{--}5\text{ nm}$) prevent direct metal-to-metal orbital overlap. In hard space vacuum ($P < 10^{-7}\text{ Torr}$), mechanical contact destroys these embrittled films without atmospheric re-oxidation. Under clamping force $F$, actual contact occurs only at microscopic surface asperities with real area $A_r \ll A_{\text{nominal}}$.

$$A_r = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \pi r_i^2 = \frac{F}{H_{\text{Meyer}}}, \qquad G_c = \frac{1}{R_c} = \frac{2 a}{\rho} + \frac{4 e^2}{h}\left(\frac{k_F a}{2}\right)^2 \mathcal{T}$$

Where $H_{\text{Meyer}}$ is the softer metal indentation hardness, $a$ is the effective micro-contact radius, $\rho$ is bulk electrical resistivity, and $\mathcal{T}$ represents electron transmission probability across clean metallic interfaces. As clamping force increases beyond the elastic limit, plastic deformation crushes asperity peaks, causing $A_r$ and electrical conductance $G$ to jump by orders of magnitude.

02. Solid-State Cohesion & Work of Adhesion

Dupré-Maugis

When two atomically pristine metallic surfaces are brought within atomic spacing ($d < 0.4\text{ nm}$), metallic bonding electrons delocalize across the boundary. The thermodynamic thermodynamic driving force is dictated by the Dupré work of adhesion:

$$W_{\text{ad}} = \gamma_1 + \gamma_2 - \gamma_{12} \approx 2 \sqrt{\gamma_1 \gamma_2}, \qquad F_{\text{pull-off}} = \frac{3}{2}\pi R\, W_{\text{ad}}$$

For titanium against beryllium copper in ultra-high vacuum, surface energies $\gamma_1, \gamma_2 \approx 1.8\text{--}2.1\text{ J/m}^2$, yielding colossal pull-off forces exceeding the maximum actuator rating of the servicing robot arm ($F_{\text{pull-off}} > 800\text{ N}$), resulting in permanent robotic arm seizure.

03. State Observer & Pre-Weld Curvature Metric

Kalman Inversion

Standard robotic load cells only track total normal force $F$, which remains constant during steady clamping. ColdWeld injects a high-frequency low-amplitude AC interrogation signal ($100\text{ kHz}, 50\text{ mV}$) through the contact to track real-time interface conductance $G(t)$.

$$\Psi_{\text{weld}} = \left.\frac{\partial G}{\partial t}\right|_{F = \text{const}} + \beta \left( \frac{\partial G}{\partial F} - \frac{1}{3}\frac{G}{F} \right) > \Psi_{\text{threshold}}$$

Under purely elastic Hertzian contact, $G \propto F^{1/3}$ so $\frac{\partial G}{\partial F} - \frac{1}{3}\frac{G}{F} = 0$. The moment plastic creep or atomic cohesion initiates, conductance surges exponentially without an increase in force ($\Psi_{\text{weld}} \gg 0$). This provides a 25 to 120 ms advance warning window before macro-scale fusion occurs.

04. Piezo Micro-Vibration Bond Fracture

Acoustic Shear

Upon detection of the $\Psi_{\text{weld}}$ tripwire, the ColdWeld control loop activates multi-axis PZT piezo actuators embedded directly behind the beryllium-copper gripper contact pads, generating tangential shear displacements at $f = 28\text{ kHz}$.

$$\tau_{\text{shear}} = G_{\text{mat}} \frac{\partial u_x}{\partial z} \ge \tau_{\text{theoretical}} \approx \frac{G_{\text{mat}}}{30}, \qquad u_x(t) = A_0 \sin(2\pi f_{\text{piezo}} t)$$

The sub-micron shear motion creates local mode-II stress concentrations at the embryonic atomic junctions. Because metallic cold-welded junctions in their first milliseconds have not undergone grain growth or recrystallization, their shear strength is easily overcome by cyclic acoustic shear, cleaving metallic bonds before diffusion bonding locks the interface.
